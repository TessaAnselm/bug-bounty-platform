# Program Selection Guide

Choosing the right program is the highest-leverage decision in bug bounty. A bad choice wastes weeks.

---

## What Makes a Good Program

### Scope
- Wildcard domains (`*.company.com`) over single assets (`app.company.com`)
- Includes APIs, mobile apps, cloud assets — not just the web app
- Explicit scope means clear authorization; vague scope means risk

### Activity signals
- Recent bounty payouts visible on the platform
- Triager response time under 2 weeks
- Public hall of fame or disclosed reports (shows they actually pay)
- Program has been active for at least 6 months

### Competition level
- Avoid the top 10 most popular programs on HackerOne — picked over
- New programs (launched in the last 3-6 months) have the most untouched surface
- Private programs pay 3-5x more — build reputation to get invited

### Payout alignment
- P1 Critical: $5,000+
- P2 High: $1,000–$5,000
- P3 Medium: $300–$1,000
- Programs that pay $50 for high severity are not worth your time

---

## Red Flags

- "We reserve the right to not pay" without clear criteria
- No disclosed reports (may not be paying)
- Very narrow scope with mostly out-of-scope exclusions
- Response time over 30 days
- No response to publicly visible reports
- Programs that duplicate-close everything (oversaturated)

---

## Where to Find Programs

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| HackerOne | Largest, most programs, highest competition on top programs |
| Bugcrowd | Strong enterprise programs |
| Intigriti | European-focused, less saturated, growing |
| YesWeHack | European, good scope variety |
| Synack | Invite-only, vetted researchers, higher payouts |
| Direct VDPs | Companies with their own security.txt or /security page |

---

## Program Selection Checklist

- [ ] Scope includes wildcard or multiple assets
- [ ] API and/or mobile in scope
- [ ] Active payouts in last 90 days
- [ ] Triage response time is reasonable
- [ ] Payout scale justifies the effort
- [ ] Not oversaturated (check duplication rate in disclosed reports)
- [ ] Aligns with your active specialization

---

## Strategy

**Start:** 1-2 programs, go deep. Spend 2-4 weeks on each before moving.

**Expand:** Once you have a paid finding, apply the same methodology to similar programs in the same industry — you've already mapped the pattern.

**Private programs:** Every paid finding and professional interaction builds the reputation that gets you invited. Treat every public program interaction as an audition.
